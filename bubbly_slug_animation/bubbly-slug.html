About this model

This is a kinematic reconstruction rendered in the style of a DNS post-processing scene, not a Navier–Stokes solution. Bubbles are tracked as Lagrangian interfaces with size-dependent slip and lift (small bubbles migrate to the wall, large ones to the core), wake entrainment and coalescence. Gas is added until α ≈ 0.30, where the bubbly regime typically gives way to slug flow. Taylor bubbles rise at UTB = 1.2 Um + 0.35 √(gD) (Nicklin, 1962) and shed small bubbles from their tails. The liquid field combines a 1/7-power turbulent profile, divergence-free synthetic turbulence, potential-flow dipoles around dispersed bubbles, and an axisymmetric stream function for each Taylor bubble that carries the falling film and a toroidal wake vortex.
